# Deforestation in Brazil's Amazon Indigenous Territories

Interactive map for a geospatial exercise on deforestation in Brazilian Amazon Indigenous Territories (ITs):
total clearing inside ITs, clearing just inside vs just outside IT edges in Pará, and designated vs undesignated
territories.

**Live map:** https://jacksontharris.github.io/amazon-it-deforestation/

Layers:
- PRODES clearing 2020–2025
- PRODES forest and clearing history
- Indigenous Territories shaded by share cleared, with a popup per territory giving PRODES and MapBiomas numbers
- Pará 10 km frontier bands

Data: INPE PRODES 2025, MapBiomas Collection 11 (via Google Earth Engine), Cartas da Terra Malha Fundiária 2026
(tenure), FUNAI territory outlines.
